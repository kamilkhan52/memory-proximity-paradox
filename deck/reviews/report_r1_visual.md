# Report R1 visual / readability review

Rendered with system Chrome (Playwright, since the CLI screenshot cuts content: 16000 px was not enough, and any full-page PNG over 16384 px tiles/repeats in headless Chrome). Desktop 1100 px = 45,640 px tall; narrow 500 px = 54,496 px. Per-figure and per-table element crops are in deck/shots/report_crops/ (d_fig01-13, n_fig01-13, d_tbl01-14, n_tbl01-14). Whole-page PNGs report_vis_*.png are unreliable beyond 16384 px; use the crops.

Checks that passed: no horizontal page scroll at 500 px (scrollWidth = 500); all 14 tables and 12 of 13 figures sit in overflow:auto containers; no SVG text under 11 px at desktop; colour semantics are consistent (blue = A, orange = B, slate = DDR, green = 3D) in Figs 1, 2, 3c, 5, 6, 7, 8a; log axes are labelled in Figs 2, 3a, 3c, 5, 9; body 16 px, table 14 px, caption 13.8 px; white background.

1. MEDIUM | Narrow width, all figures | Figures keep a fixed min-width (560-820 px) inside a scroll box at 468 px. Right-hand labels, legends and brackets are cut (Fig 2 latency text "214-394 ns..." and the CXL-hop bracket; Fig 6 legend and the Google/Meta/Mooncake strip labels; Fig 5 value labels). No scroll hint or fade. | Add a visible cue ("swipe to see full figure", edge shadow), or reflow legends and labels below the plot at <600 px. Fig 6 strips should wrap.
2. MEDIUM | Narrow, SVG text | Scaled SVG text drops to 8.8-10.7 px (about 300 text nodes below 11 px). | Raise the min-width so scale is >=0.9, or add a narrow viewBox variant for Figs 2, 5, 6.
3. MEDIUM | Provenance tags [P], [D from V], etc., and .sc chips | The tags are 8.3-10.6 px (about 150 instances), grey and monospace. They are the evidence system, yet they are the smallest and lowest-contrast text on the page. | Set to >=11.5 px and darken the grey to AA contrast.
4. MEDIUM | Navigation | The TOC (16 items) sits after the 600 px abstract and "How to read" box, about 1,270 px down. It is two columns with misaligned baselines (column 2 starts about 4 px higher, and entries 9-10 are numbered differently). It is not sticky, and there are no back-to-top links, no list of figures, and no section anchors on h3s. Over 45,000 px of content is hard to navigate. | Move the TOC to directly under the title, make it sticky or a left rail at wide widths, add a "Figures" index, and fix the column alignment.
5. LOW | Heading hierarchy | h1 then h3 "How to read this report" (no h2). h4 labels A1/B1/A2/B2 appear under h2 "Deployed versus ambitious" with no h3 between. Appendices are h2 with no visual distinction from the numbered body sections. | Make "How to read" an h2 or an unnumbered aside; add an h3 above the h4s; style appendix h2s differently.

MEDIUM | Paragraph length / walls of text | The longest paragraphs run 147-180 words (starting "Signals travel at roughly 5 ns per metre...", "Since about 2011 the DRAM $/bit decline...", "Switches. 7. LOW | Superscript citations | Reference superscripts in 16 px body text raise line boxes and make line spacing uneven (visible in the Sec 3 paragraphs around Fig 3a). | Set sup line-height:0 and position:relative; vertical-align:baseline with top:-0.4em.

Mixed alignment looks unintentional. | Either centre the captions under their figures, or align them to the figure's left edge, and keep the same caption width.
9. LOW | Fig 3a (growth) | Labels "HBM" and "GDDR5" sit on top of the trend line, "NVLink 4.0-" touches the line end, and the "R10000" label hangs under the y=1 tick.
