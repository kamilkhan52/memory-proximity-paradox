04 · Pressure 2: capacity cost → B

Capacity cost now bites: $/bit declines slowed ~2.5–3× and memory reached 37% of Meta's rack TCO.

Bits stopped getting cheap fast while core counts kept growing, and much of the memory bought sits idle.

Memory share of rack TCO and power across six Meta generations (data labels as printed). TPP, ASPLOS'23, Fig. 3.

$/bit decline:~33–37%/yr before ~2010; ~12–15%/yr since (chart: slide 9).
Capacity per unit of CPU performance:fell ~60–70% in 10 years.
Idle bytes:32% of Google fleet memory is cold at 120 s; 55–80% of Meta app memory is idle per 2 min.
